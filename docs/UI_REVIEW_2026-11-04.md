## Findings and concrete outcomes

### 1. Readability and theme

The current palette makes many secondary controls hard to distinguish. In one normal 1480×960 reader state, 51 of 78 sampled visible text-style/color combinations fell below their size-based contrast benchmark. The raw sample includes separators and decoration; it is not a count of 51 separate accessibility violations. Disabled controls, gradients, transparent PDF text and clipped content were excluded where identifiable. Primary book text and the main title performed better than secondary labels.

Meaningful examples: **Go to audio 2.77:1 at 9 px**, **Follow audio 2.86:1 at 9 px**, audio-page status **2.99:1 at 9 px**, Speech cue **3.04:1 at 10 px**, and a page-label detail **2.25:1 at 8 px**. Normal text's reference minimum is 4.5:1; large text uses 3:1. These are benchmark comparisons, not a full conformance certification. [W3C contrast guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html).

Recommended direction: parchment surfaces, near-black ink for book text, darker secondary labels and restrained walnut/bronze accents. Use semantic colors for text, surfaces, borders, focus, selected state and speech cues so a future theme change reaches every screen. Increase frequently used labels toward 11–12 px while retaining the compact layout. Preserve the separate Highlight/Underline choice. Keep original PDF page colors faithful; theme the surrounding application and reflowable reading surface.

The concept screenshot illustrates palette and hierarchy only. It is not a finished theme, has not had every screen/hover/focus state audited, and is not installed. Several voice avatars were made neutral in the concept; a finished design should keep useful voice distinctions.

### 2. A hidden library search can make the current book look empty

Reproduction: open the synthetic Navigation Review PDF → My library → search for `Navigation Review` → open that same active book. The reader shows **zero passages** and “No passages found,” while the document search box is hidden. Its text contains no title match. Choosing a different book can clear the state through the active-ID effect; reopening the same book does not.

The shared `search` value is used for both library titles and document contents. Give those searches separate state, or explicitly clear document search when selecting a book. A document filter should remain visible whenever it affects the reader. [Evidence](C:/Users/Hasan/Documents/Cadence-1.0.12-Migration-2026-11-01-Windows-x64/test-results/ui-review-2026-11-04/packaged-library-search-reopen.png). Relevant implementation: `src/App.tsx`, shared `search`, `selectDocument` and the active-ID reset effect.

### 3. Restoration can hide the actual heading under the page marker

On a fresh isolated startup, the welcome book's first heading “THE LISTENING ROOM” is restored behind the sticky Section 1 marker. The heading occupies y=140–188.5; the marker covers y=146.5–185.5. Hit testing the center of the first word reaches the marker instead of the word. Scrolling to the physical top makes the word visible and clickable again. Both builds reproduce it.

Restore and explicit text targets with an allowance for the sticky marker, rather than anchoring the passage only 12 px below the viewport. Check actual visibility/hit testing after settling, including headings, same-page chapter jumps and font-size changes. Keep PDF canvas restoration and independent audio positions unchanged. [Evidence](C:/Users/Hasan/Documents/Cadence-1.0.12-Migration-2026-11-01-Windows-x64/test-results/ui-review-2026-11-04/packaged-startup-heading-obscured.png). Relevant implementation: `src/useReaderResume.ts` restoration scroll, `src/App.tsx` target scroll, `.original-page-marker` in `src/styles.css`.

### 4. Bookmark creation and bookmark opening follow different notions of “current”

With browsing on page 4 and audio on page 1, the toolbar bookmark stores passage 0 on page 1. Its label says “Bookmark current passage,” which does not make the audio basis apparent. More seriously, clicking that page-1 bookmark card returns to the reader still showing page 4; the audio checkpoint changes, but the intended bookmarked text is not revealed.

Make bookmark-card selection explicitly reveal its saved passage, cancelling automatic restoration for that user action. Clarify whether the main bookmark action saves the viewed passage or audio passage; ideally offer an obvious reading bookmark and a separate audio-position action. Opening a bookmark should not start speech automatically. Relevant implementation: `toggleBookmark`, bookmark-card click handler and `updateBookmarkedPosition` in `src/App.tsx`.

### 5. Space after a word click restarts the paragraph

Click `calm` in Reading view. Speech highlighting starts at “calm story through the trees.” Focus stays on the enclosing passage button. Press Space: the player remains in its playing state and the highlighted range changes to the whole paragraph beginning “On page 1 passage 1…”. The native button activation starts the paragraph again; the global playback shortcut ignores focused buttons. No new synthesis request is required because the earlier full sentence is cached.

Handle mouse-started reader focus so the documented pause shortcut works, while retaining Enter/Space activation when a keyboard user intentionally focuses a passage. Do not globally hijack Space on every button, input or select. Relevant implementation: `src/ReadingSection.tsx` passage activation and `src/App.tsx` keyboard handler.

### 6. Original-page PDF tools imply behavior they do not provide

In PDF Document view, clicking Larger text twice and Change typeface leaves the canvas display size at **616×797**, unchanged. Those settings alter the Reading view, but no contextual explanation is shown. Searching `On page 5 passage 7` while viewing page 4 reports **1 passage**, keeps page 4 visible, and creates no search highlight or match navigation on the original PDF.

Provide fit-width/zoom controls for original pages and usable search results that jump to and mark the matched page. Typeface changes should be clearly specific to reflowed text. Until native PDF search is implemented, an explicit switch to Reading view would make the result useful. Avoid silent no-effect controls. [Evidence](C:/Users/Hasan/Documents/Cadence-1.0.12-Migration-2026-11-01-Windows-x64/test-results/ui-review-2026-11-04/packaged-native-search.png). Relevant implementation: reading toolbar in `src/App.tsx`; `src/PdfPage.tsx` has no font/search input.

### 7. Removal has no recovery step

A single click on the small trash control removes the synthetic pasted note immediately. No confirmation or Undo action appears; the stored note is gone. This is current intended behavior, but a pasted note may have no external original file to recover from. The message about an unchanged original file is not useful for that case.

Prefer a short Undo window with a retained record until expiry, or a clear confirmation for removal when Undo is unavailable. Protect bookmarks and text without putting confirmation prompts on ordinary reading actions. Any retention/storage change should be reviewed before implementation under the repository rules.

### 8. Typeface and text size reset

After choosing 21 px sans-serif and reloading, the reader returns to 19 px serif. Voice, speed and cue preferences persist, but the user's readability setup does not. Save and validate the two small display preferences; do not reset the reading position when applying them. Relevant implementation: initial `fontSize` and `fontTheme` state in `src/App.tsx`.

### 9. The expanded blender exceeds the voice panel at minimum window size

At supported 1000×700, expanding Blend voices produces a panel with **524 px client height and 721 px scroll height**, with `overflow: visible`. Use blend is at y=588.5–618.5, behind the player that begins around y=578. The voice list and footer extend below the panel. Scrolling only the voice list cannot recover the whole expanded form.

Make the panel's main contents scroll, retain a reachable close button, and avoid a mandatory non-shrinking form plus non-shrinking footer. Keep temporary full-screen drawers usable too. [Evidence](C:/Users/Hasan/Documents/Cadence-1.0.12-Migration-2026-11-01-Windows-x64/test-results/ui-review-2026-11-04/source-small-blender.png).

Simulated 125% renderer zoom on 1000×700 additionally produces an 800×560 CSS viewport against a 650 px minimum app height. The player initially falls below the viewport and can be recovered by outer scrolling. Settings/Help remain beyond the reachable body bottom in the focused test, even at maximum outer scroll. Treat this as a scaling stress finding, not proof about every Windows DPI configuration. Replace rigid minimum heights with layouts that keep essential controls reachable before supporting a general UI-scale control.

### 10. Several controls require precise clicking

Examples at normal zoom: Paste text 20×20, navigation close 20×20, heading expand 17×30, small text buttons 22×27, and full-screen page/skip arrows 20 px wide. Range tracks are visually 3 px high; their native thumbs can have a larger hit region, so the track dimensions alone do not establish a pointer-target failure.

Prefer 28–32 px hit areas for frequently used controls, preserve clear focus outlines, and remove duplicate decorative micro-labels before expanding the overall header. WCAG's 24×24 minimum has spacing, inline, native-control and other exceptions; geometry alone does not decide conformance. [W3C target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html).

## What already works

Original PDF rendering, exact-word starts, independent browsing/audio, optional Follow audio, full-screen controls, panel persistence, exclusive speech cue selection, real offline playback/export and restart checkpoints passed the routine checks. Dialog Escape closes correctly, whitespace-only pasted text is rejected, and focus returns to the Paste text button. The paste dialog initially focuses Close rather than its title field; focusing the title would be a low-effort polish item, below the ranked work above.

## Suggested implementation order

First fix hidden search, restored-target visibility, bookmark reveal and the Space interaction; add regression checks that assert visible outcomes rather than only stored state or bounding positions. Then implement the selected parchment/ink theme together with font persistence and larger important labels. Follow with proper original-page PDF tools, removal recovery and short-window panel resilience. Keep word-level speech alignment as a separate future enhancement already discussed with the user.

Ranking expresses overall benefit; this order groups small behavioral fixes before the visual pass to make subsequent UI testing clearer.
